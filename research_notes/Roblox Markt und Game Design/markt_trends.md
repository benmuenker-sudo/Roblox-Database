# Roblox Game Market 2025–2026: Top Games, Breakout Hits, Genres and Trends

Research date: 2026-10-24. Method: WebSearch only (WebFetch/curl unavailable; the session's shared web-search budget ran out after ~40 queries, so some planned follow-ups could not be run, see Gaps). Source quality is tagged as follows: **[OFFICIAL]** = Roblox IR/SEC filings, **[PRESS]** = games-industry or general press, **[TRACKER]** = third-party CCU trackers (RoWatcher, Rolimons, RoVitals, Statroblox, Profitable.app), **[BLOG]** = SEO/guide sites or low-quality aggregators. CCU = concurrent users. Tracker numbers often differ by 5–30% from each other, and live snapshots are only accurate for the moment they were taken.

## 1. Which experiences have the highest CCU and visits in 2026?

## 2. Breakout hits 2025–2026: launch, peak, mechanics, decline

### Takeaway
The 2025 hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights, Plants vs Brainrots) worked through **simple, AFK-friendly accumulation loops plus social/PvP stealing plus weekly "admin abuse"/limited-time events**, with peaks timed to Saturday updates. They declined steeply. Steal a Brainrot is down about 99% from peak. Grow a Garden 2 (June 2026) peaked at only about 0.93–0.98M. In 2026 the pattern repeated with "Steal An Egg" (pet stealing/hatching, 14.2M peak in Sept 2026).

### Inferences
- **Formula of the 2025–26 mega-hits:** (1) a low-skill, mobile-friendly accumulation/idle loop (plant, buy, hatch), (2) social conflict through stealing/raiding, which forces players to return and log in, (3) rarity tiers and trading, (4) weekly Saturday updates and "admin abuse" live events that create FOMO peaks, (5) a meme-able TikTok/YouTube aesthetic (brainrot), and (6) crossovers between hit games.
- **Half-life:** viral hits lose most of their CCU within months (Steal a Brainrot -99% within about 9–12 months of its peak; Blue Lock Rivals about -96%; Grow a Garden 2 roughly -99% within 2 months). Sequels do not reliably bring back the original's audience (Grow a Garden 2 peaked at about 4% of Grow a Garden).
- "Steal a ___" became a template: Steal a Brainrot (2025), then Grow a Garden 2 with a night-stealing mechanic, then Steal An Egg (2026).

- The developer of Steal An Egg is unclear (Misfits claims portfolio involvement, and tracker metadata is truncated).

## 3. Genre shares and growth; brainrot; viral vs evergreen

### Takeaway
Simulation is by far the largest genre by CCU (about 36% share in Oct 2026, Statroblox). Roleplay & Avatar Sim follows at about 12%, then Action at about 8% and Shooter at about 4%. Shooters and Survival are growing week over week, while Simulation and Roleplay are slightly declining. Brainrot is a cross-genre aesthetic, not a genre. It peaked in 2025 and is cooling but not dead.

### Cited Findings
- Statroblox, October 2026: Simulation 5.1M players (35.6%), Roleplay & Avatar Sim 1.7M (11.9%), Action 7.9%, Shooter 540K (3.8%), out of 14,058,000 tracked players **[TRACKER]** — [Statroblox genres](https://statroblox.com/genres)
- 7-day growth (Oct 2026): Survival +13% (fastest), Shooter +4.5%, Roleplay -5.6%, Simulation -7.9% **[TRACKER]** — [Statroblox guide](https://statroblox.com/guides/most-popular-roblox-genres)
- Concentration: Roleplay has the most players per game (961), and its top game holds 36% of the genre. Simulation has 14,672 games at 345 players per game. Shooter has 859 games, and its top game holds 34% **[TRACKER]** — [Statroblox](https://statroblox.com/genres)
- Rolearn (Q4 2025 to Feb 2026): Roleplay plus Simulator are more than 35% of active experiences and about 28% of platform CCU. ## 4. Platform metrics 2025–2026 (official)

### Takeaway
Roblox peaked in Q3 2025 (151.5M DAU, 39.6B hours, $1.92B bookings, +70% YoY). In 2026 DAU fell three quarters in a row (144M Q4'25, 132M Q1'26, 123M Q2'26). Bookings growth slowed sharply to +8% in Q2'26, and Q3'26 guidance is -14% to -18% YoY. Causes named: the high 2025 base, the Russia ban (Dec 2025), age-check headwinds, a deliberate algorithm shift toward retention, and lower per-hour monetization among young US/CA users. Growth is concentrated in APAC (Japan, India) and among 18+ users.

### Inferences
- **Opportunities for 2027:** (a) mid-core experiences for players 17+/18–34 (competitive shooters like Rivals, asymmetric horror like Forsaken, co-op survival like 99 Nights) with high retention and better monetization; (b) evergreen design with trading/collection economies and a seasonal event cadence (the MM2 model) instead of a pure viral spike; (c) localization for Japan, India and LATAM; (d) "steal/raid" social mechanics remain a proven viral accelerator but need a retention layer (progression, guilds, base building), otherwise you get a -99% curve.
- **Risks:** the brainrot aesthetic is saturating (rapid declines among imitators); age checks reduce chat and social virality; lower monetization among young US users.
- The market for "game flipping" (stake purchases in hit games by DoBig, Splitting Point, Super League, Misfits) is becoming professionalized. Hits increasingly come from portfolio studios with live-ops capacity rather than solo developers alone, although the original hits (Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot) started with very small teams or individuals.
