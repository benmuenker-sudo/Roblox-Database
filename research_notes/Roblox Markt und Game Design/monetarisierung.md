## 1. DevEx: rates, minimums, eligibility, payout totals

### Takeaway
The standard DevEx rate has been **$0.0038 per Robux since Sept 5, 2025**, up 8.5% from $0.0035. Robux earned before that date still cash out at $0.0035. A higher **US 18+ rate of $0.0054** applies from **June 8, 2026** to qualifying spend by age-checked US adults. The minimum cash-out is 30,000 earned Robux. Creators earned **$1.503B through DevEx in 2025**, up 63% from $922.8M in 2024. More than 23,500 creators were paid, and the top 1,000 averaged $1.3M each.

### Cited Findings
**Rates**
- [OFFICIAL] The DevEx rate rose from $0.0035 to $0.0038 per Robux on Sept 5, 2025 (announced at RDC 2025). Roblox's wording: "Creators will now earn 8.5% more when turning Earned Robux into cash." — [Roblox on X](https://x.com/Roblox/status/1964021019049676912); [Roblox Newsroom RDC 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-rdc-2025)
- [OFFICIAL] Robux earned before the change still cash out at $0.0035. The cash-out order is: 18+-rate Robux first, then Robux earned before Sept 5, 2025, then the rest. — [Roblox Help: Developer Exchange](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/13061189551124-Developer-Exchange-Help-and-Information-Page) (search snippet)
- [COMMUNITY, conflict] One developer said they cashed out about 600k Robux in Nov 2025 and were paid at the old $0.0035 rate. This looks like a single account's case, or a result of the cash-out order (older Robux go first). — [DevForum "DevEx rate Confusion"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/devex-rate-confusion/4717348)
- [OFFICIAL] **US 18+ DevEx rate:** $0.0054 per Robux ($162 per 30,000 Robux), announced Apr 30, 2026 and effective June 8, 2026. It is 42% higher than the standard rate ($0.38 to $0.54 per 100 Robux). It applies to in-game spend by players who age-checked as 18+ through facial age estimation or government ID **and** whose economic location is the US. — [Roblox Newsroom Apr 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42); [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091); [Creator Docs: U.S. 18+ exchange rate](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate)
- [OFFICIAL, conflict on scope] The newsroom says the 18+ rate covers "game passes, Robux subscriptions, select in-game items, and private servers." The DevForum post says in-game avatar items do **not** qualify. — same sources as above
- [COMMUNITY] Some third-party sources say only R15 games qualify for the 18+ rate, and that mixed R6/R15 games do not. Unverified. — [devex.gg](https://devex.gg/18-plus-devex-rate); [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-to-raise-developer-exchange-rate-for-games-aimed-at-18-plus-players-as-adult-cohort-surges/)

**Minimum and eligibility**
- [OFFICIAL] The minimum cash-out is **30,000 Earned Robux**, about $114 at $0.0038 or $105 at the legacy rate. — [Creator Docs: Developer Exchange](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange); [DevEx Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005718246-Developer-Exchange-Terms-of-Use)
- [OFFICIAL] Requirements are: age **13+**, a **Roblox-verified email**, a valid **DevEx portal account** (payments run through Tipalti), a **W-9 or W-8** tax form on file, compliance with the Terms of Use and Community Standards (good standing), and at least 30,000 earned Robux. Roblox may change these at any time. — [DevEx Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005718246-Developer-Exchange-Terms-of-Use); [DevEx Overview](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203314100-Developer-Exchange-DevEx-Overview-How-to-Submit-Requirements)
- [OFFICIAL] **Premium is no longer required** for DevEx (changed in early 2022). — [DevForum "Developer Exchange Program Requirements Changes"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/developer-exchange-program-requirements-changes/1651282)
- [COMMUNITY, outdated] Some 2026 SEO guides still list a 100,000 Robux minimum and a Premium requirement. These contradict the official pages. — [rolearn.dev](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/roblox-devex-requirements-2026/)

**Payout totals**
- [OFFICIAL] Developer exchange fees were **$1,503.1M in FY2025** vs **$922.8M in FY2024** (+63%). Roblox attributes the increase to bookings growth, the **Creator Rewards** launch, and the 8.5% rate increase from Sept 5, 2025. — [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- [OFFICIAL] **More than 23,500 creators** took part in DevEx and were paid fiat in 2025. — [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- [OFFICIAL] The **top 1,000 creators averaged $1.3M** for full-year 2025, up more than 50% YoY. The Q3 2025 letter gave a trailing-twelve-month average of $1.1M. — [Q4 2025 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Q4-2025-Shareholder-Letter.pdf); [Q4 2025 call transcript](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/RBLX/pressreleases/59554/roblox-rblx-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL via secondary] Creators earned a record **$477M in Q4 2025**, up 70% YoY. — [Lindsey Gamble on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@lindseygamble_/post/DUf3DVrkwJ5/in-creators-have-earned-over-billion-through-robloxs-developer-exchange-dev-ex)
- [OFFICIAL] Creators earned more than **$1B through DevEx in the 12 months ending June 30, 2025**. — [Roblox Newsroom RDC 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-rdc-2025)
- [OFFICIAL] **More than 100 developers earned over $1M** in the trailing 12 months as of the Q1 2025 release (CEO statement). — [Q1 2025 8-K Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000117/rblx-20250331xexhibit991.htm)
- [ESTIMATE/secondary] One social post put the median creator's earnings at about $1,500. Unverified. — [endsights.com](https://endsights.com/roblox-creator-economy) / social (via search summary)

### Inferences
- FY2025 DevEx of $1.503B against FY2025 bookings of $6.8B ([Naavik State of UGC 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)) means about **22% of bookings went to creators as cash**. That is a useful planning figure for the share of a player's dollar a creator actually receives after Robux pricing, the marketplace fee, and the DevEx rate.
- The cash-out order means creators with large pre-Sept 2025 balances will keep seeing $0.0035 payouts until that legacy balance is used up. This likely explains the DevForum "confusion" posts.
- The 18+ rate is a deliberate incentive toward adult-targeted, high-fidelity (R15) games, and toward game passes, private servers and subscriptions over avatar items.

### Gaps
- No official full-year 2025 count of creators earning more than $1M or more than $100k was found. The latest official $1M+ count is "over 100" (TTM, Q1 2025).
- No Q1–Q3 2026 DevEx totals were found. The Q2 2026 results (July 30, 2026) did not turn up creator payout figures in search. Q3 2026 results had likely not been released as of Oct 24, 2026.
- The help-center page could not be fetched directly to confirm the cash-out-order wording.

## 2. Marketplace fees, revenue shares, and pricing tools

### Takeaway
Creators keep **70%** of game pass and developer product sales; Roblox takes a 30% marketplace fee. Experience subscriptions pay 70%. Creator Store models and plugins pay **100% net** of taxes and processing, sold in USD through Stripe. The biggest 2025–26 changes were these:
- **Regional Pricing** came to passes (April 2025) and later to developer products.
- Regional pricing became the default for all passes (March 30, 2026).
- Regional Pricing and Price Optimization merged into **Managed Pricing** (around June 15, 2026).
- Cross-game pass and product sales ended (May 29, 2026).
- A contested **2D clothing overhaul (July 2026)** cut the base marketplace creator share for 2D items from 70% to 30% and added upload and publish fees.

### Cited Findings
**Shares per product type**
- [COMMUNITY, consistent with long-standing official docs] Game passes and developer products: creators keep **70%** and Roblox takes a 30% fee. For example, a 500 Robux pass nets 350 Robux. — [creation.dev pricing guide](https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-price-game-passes-roblox); [ExitLag guide](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/how-to-make-a-gamepass-on-roblox/). The official fee page was not fetched.
- [OFFICIAL] **Experience Subscriptions:** priced either in local currency at fixed tiers of **$2.99 / $4.99 / $7.99 / $9.99 / $14.99** per month, or in **Robux at any amount of 49 Robux or more**. Robux pricing was announced around RDC 2025, and regional pricing is enabled for all Robux subscriptions. The search summary describes the payout as "70% of subscription earnings each month, or 70% in the first month and 100% afterward." The exact split by payment type should be checked on the docs page. The feature is unavailable in some countries. — [Creator Docs: Subscriptions](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/subscriptions); [DevForum "Let your players pay for subscriptions within experiences in Robux"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/let-your-players-pay-for-subscriptions-within-experiences-in-robux/4552995)
- [OFFICIAL] **Creator Store (models, plugins):** creators "earn 100% of net proceeds"; only taxes and payment processing fees are deducted. Prices are in **USD**, onboarding runs through **Stripe**, and the **minimum plugin price is $4.99**. The USD/100% model applies to models and plugins only. — [Creator Docs: Creator Store / selling](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/creator-store); [Tubefilter Jan 2024](https://tubefilter.com/2024/01/29/roblox-creator-store-fees/)
- [OFFICIAL] **Transfers API** (launched May 4, 2026), which replaced peer-to-peer Robux transfers through cross-game passes: the **creator earns a 10% commission and the recipient gets 90%**. **Cross-game sales** of passes and developer products were **disabled May 29, 2026**. — [DevForum: Disabling Cross-Game Sales… Transfers API](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/disabling-cross-game-sales-of-passes-and-dev-products-and-introducing-the-transfers-api/4618396)

**UGC / avatar items (2D clothing overhaul, July 2026)**
- [OFFICIAL] Roblox's DevForum post "Unifying 2D and 3D Avatar Item Publishing" first proposed (July 7, 2026) **200 Robux per upload** and a publishing advance from **600 Robux**. It revised this (around July 13, 2026) to **80 Robux upload** for both 2D and 3D, and a **10 Robux publishing advance for 2D**, refundable from the platform share as the item sells. — [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/building-stronger-protections-and-a-more-consistent-marketplace-unifying-2d-and-3d-avatar-item-publishing/4722717); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/backlash-clothing-changes-unifying-requirements-2d-3d); [ugcraft.ai](https://www.ugcraft.ai/posts/roblox-2d-clothing-update-2026)
- [ESTIMATE/press, single source] The base marketplace creator share for **2D items fell from 70% to 30%** (July 14, 2026), and the in-experience creator share went from 60% to 30%. Roblox says the **total paid to the developer community did not shrink**; the split shifted toward game owners where items are sold. Creators objected strongly. — [tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/roblox-2d-creator-revenue-share-cut-2026/); [DevForum "Revert Changes to the 2D Clothing Update"](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/revert-changes-to-the-2d-clothing-update/4732289)
- [COMMUNITY] Selling 2D items now reportedly requires an active **Roblox Plus or Premium 1000/2200** membership plus ID verification or a linked parent account. Roblox says it is not phasing out 2D clothing. — [ugcraft.ai](https://www.ugcraft.ai/posts/roblox-2d-clothing-update-2026); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/backlash-clothing-changes-unifying-requirements-2d-3d)

**Regional pricing, price optimization, Managed Pricing**
- [OFFICIAL] **Regional Pricing for Passes** launched to all creators as opt-in in **April 2025**. The creator sets a default price and Roblox sets regional prices automatically. **VPN users see the default (highest) price.** Roblox said **Price Optimization**, the earlier automatic price-testing tool, "has already helped eligible creators grow their earnings by 4%." — [DevForum: Introducing Regional Pricing for Passes](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-regional-pricing-for-passes/3621382); [Creator Docs: Price optimization](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/price-optimization)
- [COMMUNITY, conflict] A DevForum user quoted the docs as saying regional prices "never go below 30% of the default price." Other users reported discounts of about 60%. — [DevForum thread, later pages](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-regional-pricing-for-passes/3621382?page=4)
- [OFFICIAL] **Regional Pricing for Developer Products** was announced separately. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-regional-pricing-for-developer-products/3971235) (exact date not captured; thread ID suggests late 2025)
- [OFFICIAL] Regional pricing was **enabled by default for all passes starting March 30** (2026), with no action needed. Developer products still required opt-in at that point. — [DevForum: Enabling Regional Prices for All Passes](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/enabling-regional-prices-for-all-passes-to-grow-your-global-audience/4471199)
- [OFFICIAL via secondary] **Managed Pricing** launched around **June 15, 2026** (DevForum t/4684738). It unifies Regional Pricing and Price Optimization and "automatically runs tests that keep your games' prices competitive" for passes and developer products. Existing Regional Pricing items migrate automatically, new games and items enroll by default with item-level opt-out, and creators can turn it off. Subscriptions and private servers are excluded but still auto-regionalized. A **Dynamic Price Check** tool helps catch hard-coded prices in UI. — [Bloxy News on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@bloxy.news/post/DZn3MbEgRAy/introducing-managed-pricing-a-new-system-for-roblox-creators-that-unifies/); [RoUtility on X](https://x.com/RoUtilityNews/status/2066644193200541795); [zehn-studio26 blog](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/managed-pricing/)

### Inferences
- Since mid-2026, prices on Roblox are partly managed by the platform. Hard-coded Robux prices in UI break under Managed Pricing, so games should read live prices from MarketplaceService.
- Ending cross-game sales of passes and products closes the donation/transfer loophole. The 10% creator commission on the Transfers API is a new, small revenue line for "donation" and trading games.
- The 2D clothing change shifts economics from clothing makers toward the game owners whose games sell items in-experience.

### Gaps
- The official marketplace-fee page was not retrieved to confirm 70% for passes and products, the UGC 3D item split (historically 30% to creator, 40% to the game owner for in-experience sales, from background knowledge, not verified this session), or the private server split.
- The exact payout wording for Experience Subscriptions by payment type is unclear.
- No official figure was found for the average revenue lift from Managed Pricing. One third-party claim of +4–10% was truncated and not used.

## 3. Revenue streams beyond direct sales

### Takeaway
On **July 24, 2025**, Roblox replaced **Engagement-Based (Premium) Payouts** and the **Creator Affiliate** program with **Creator Rewards**, an uncapped program with two parts:
- **Daily Engagement:** 5 Robux per Active Spender who plays 10 minutes or more.
- **Audience Expansion:** 35% of a new or returning user's first $100 of Robux purchases.

**Roblox Plus** ($4.99/month, from April 30, 2026) replaced Premium for new subscribers and adds referral and private-server payouts. Other monetization channels are **Rewarded Video Ads** (April 2025, with Google), **Commerce APIs / Shopify** for physical goods (May 2025, US 13+), and the 18+ DevEx rate.

### Cited Findings
**Creator Rewards (replaced Premium/Engagement-Based Payouts)**
- [OFFICIAL] The Engagement-Based Payouts program ran **through July 23, 2025**. **Creator Rewards replaced it on July 24, 2025.** — [Roblox 10-Q Q1 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028904/rblx-20260331.htm); [Creator Docs: Engagement-based payouts (deprecated)](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts)
- [OFFICIAL] Creator Rewards replaced both EBP and the **Creator Affiliate** program, and payouts are **uncapped**. — [DevForum: Introducing Creator Rewards](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628); [DevForum: Creator Rewards is Live](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-rewards-is-live/3838257)
- [OFFICIAL] **Daily Engagement Reward** at launch: **5 Robux** when an **Active Spender** spends at least **10 minutes** in the experience in a day, provided it is one of the first three experiences that user launches that day. Active Spenders are a much larger group than Premium subscribers. — [DevForum: Introducing Creator Rewards](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628)
- [OFFICIAL] **Audience Expansion Reward:** a **35% revenue share on the first $100 of Robux purchases** made by **new or returning users** (inactive at least 60 days) during their **first two months**. Credit is applied when the user arrives through the creator's links or by searching the experience name, and then plays at least 10 minutes. — [DevForum: Introducing Creator Rewards](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/406941/roblox-to-reward-creators-for-attracting-players.html)
- [COMMUNITY, conflict] Later sources describe a "60-day" window instead of "two months," and require the experience to average 100+ DAU over the 60 days after the join date. — [Roblox Fandom Wiki: Creator Rewards](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Creator_Rewards); [obby.fun guide, Sept 2026](https://www.obby.fun/blog/roblox-creator-rewards-guide)
- [OFFICIAL] The 10-K names the Creator Rewards launch as one driver of the 63% rise in 2025 DevEx fees. — [10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)

**Roblox Plus (April 30, 2026)**
- [OFFICIAL] **Roblox Plus** costs **$4.99/month** and replaced the three-tier Premium subscription for new sign-ups. Roblox pays for the Plus item discounts itself, so creators earn the same per item. — [Roblox Newsroom: Introducing Roblox Plus](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/introducing-roblox-plus-subscription); [DevForum: Introducing Roblox Plus](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-roblox-plus/4567894)
- [OFFICIAL via press] Creators using the new API earn **250 Robux per month for each Plus subscriber referred from their game**, for up to 3 months. Plus subscribers get free access to paid private servers, and creators earn **up to 100 Robux per Plus subscriber who spends 60+ cumulative minutes in the game's paid private server over 30 days**. — [Tubefilter Apr 13, 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/13/roblox-plus-creator-payouts/)
- [COMMUNITY] Plus Robux bundles are reportedly $8.99 for 500, $12.99 for 1,000 and $21.99 for 2,000 Robux/month. Legacy Premium tiers were $4.99/$9.99/$19.99. — [gghubs](https://www.gghubs.com/en/blog/roblox-robux-prices-plus-worth-it); [playtoearn](https://playtoearn.com/news/roblox-plus-launches-april-30-at-499-monthly-replacing-premium-with-discount-first-model)

**Ads**
- [OFFICIAL] **Rewarded Video Ads** launched in April 2025 (announced at IAB Playfronts) with **Google** as the programmatic partner. Eligible creators earn Robux for every ad shown, and control the prompt, frequency, reward value and reward type. Average completion rate is **above 80%**, with some experiences above 90%. Roblox says "users who view ads spend more Robux within experiences." — [Roblox Newsroom Apr 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-scales-video-ads-partners-with-google); [Variety](https://variety.com/2025/gaming/news/roblox-google-rewarded-video-ads-product-1236352518/)
- [OFFICIAL] Access was expanded to more creators in mid-2025. — [DevForum: More Creators Can Now Use Rewarded Video Ads](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/more-creators-can-now-use-rewarded-video-ads/3838678)
- [OFFICIAL via press] At RDC 2025, Roblox teased more ad placement types, and **Video and Image Billboards** through Google "in the coming months." — [Yahoo/Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/roblox-plans-expanded-placement-options-133338923.html)

**Commerce (physical goods)**
- [OFFICIAL] **Commerce APIs with Shopify** (May 2025) let eligible creators and brands sell **physical products inside experiences**. Available in **all-ages experiences to US users aged 13+**. Roblox's case study: studio **Twin Atlas** made six-figure commerce revenue in its first weeks, with about 90% of orders placed in-game. A separate **Approved Merchandiser Program** links real-world purchases to digital avatar items. — [Roblox Newsroom May 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/05/roblox-launches-shopify-integration-amp-licensing-program); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/15/roblox-now-lets-creators-sell-physical-products-within-their-experiences)

**IP licensing**
- [OFFICIAL] At RDC 2025, Roblox announced a licensing program giving creators access to IP from Mattel, Kodansha, Lionsgate and others. It also stated a goal of capturing 10% of global gaming content revenue. — [Roblox Newsroom RDC 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-rdc-2025); [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20250905587466/en/Roblox-Unveils-AI-Monetization-and-Performance-Innovations-for-Creators)

### Inferences
- Creator Rewards pays creators for engaging spenders, not Premium members. That rewards session length among paying users, and off-platform marketing through links (Audience Expansion), more than the old Premium-playtime model did.
- The Plus private-server payout (up to 100 Robux per subscriber) makes paid private servers a more important feature to offer, since Plus subscribers get them free and creators are still compensated.

### Gaps
- No official total for Creator Rewards payouts and no 2026 rule changes were found (search budget exhausted).
- No per-impression or eCPM rate for Rewarded Video or Immersive Ads was found. Roblox does not publish one.
- No revenue split or fee terms were found for Commerce APIs or Shopify.
- Paid Access (paid entry) price ranges and the USD paid-access option were not researched (budget exhausted).

## 4. Benchmarks: conversion, ARPDAU/ARPPU, price points, top games

### Takeaway
Roblox publishes **no numeric conversion or ARPPU benchmarks**. Its analytics dashboard compares games to "similar experiences" instead. Platform-level figures for Q2 2026: 123M DAU, 27M average monthly unique payers, and $12.66 bookings per DAU per quarter. Third-party rules of thumb: **1–5% payer conversion**, ARPPU of about 100–300 Robux in tycoons, and tiered passes from 25 to 2,000 Robux. Top-game revenue figures are **all estimates**. Grow a Garden made about $12M in May 2025, and Steal a Brainrot about $11M/month at its August 2025 peak (both from Creator Exchange). Both have since declined sharply, and the 2026 bookings slowdown is attributed to engagement shifting away from these high-monetizing viral games.

### Cited Findings
**Platform KPIs [OFFICIAL]**
- Q2 2026 (reported July 30, 2026): revenue **$1.5B (+36%)**, bookings **$1.6B (+8%**, low end of guidance), DAU **123M (+10% YoY)**, hours **29B (+5%)**, average monthly unique payers **27M (+15%)**. The CFO blamed a greater-than-expected shift of engagement from high-monetizing 2025 viral games to new and evergreen games with lower hourly monetization. Q3 2026 guidance: **bookings −14% to −18% YoY**. — [Q2 2026 8-K Ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm); [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/roblox-q2-2026-slides-revenue-rises-36-as-bookings-growth-stalls-93CH-4826381); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/roblox-corp-rblx-q2-2026-050249366.html)
- ABPDAU in Q2 2026 was **$12.66 (−2% YoY)** per secondary sources. Another source gives $13.35, which may be a regional figure (conflict). ## 5. Design best practices, ethics, kid safety, legal and regulatory

### Takeaway
Roblox's binding rules center on the **Paid Random Items policy**, which requires full outcome lists and numerical odds summing to 100%, and covers luck boosts and pity systems, plus trading restrictions for ineligible users. Its guidance otherwise stresses benchmarking against similar games. Legal pressure in 2025–26 is mostly about **child safety**: suits from at least 9 US states, an MDL, and Florida criminal subpoenas. Monetization-specific cases are limited to gambling via Robux casino sites (2023, largely dismissed), unauthorized purchases by children (class action against Roblox and Stripe), and a "child labor" creator-pay suit (2024).

### Cited Findings
**Official policy and guidance**
- [OFFICIAL] **Paid random items** bought directly or indirectly with Robux, including through paid in-game currency, must show **all possible outcomes and their actual numerical odds**. This covers capsules, enhancement and combination items, and **probability modifiers such as luck boosts, pity systems and rate-up scrolls**. Percentages must **sum to exactly 100%**. A pop-up is allowed for long lists, but its trigger needs a descriptive label; a bare "(i)" icon is not enough. The page was last updated Oct 15, 2026. — [Creator Docs: Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)
- [OFFICIAL] Older policy text: for indirect randomness (for example, buying a seed with Robux and planting it for a random result), odds must be shown **before** planting. This is directly relevant to Grow a Garden-style designs. — [Developer Hub: Virtual items policy](https://developer.roblox.com/zh-cn/articles/virtual-items-policy)
- [OFFICIAL] Creators must not let users the API marks ineligible trade the results of paid random items or other paid items. Seen on the Spanish-language page; the English page should be checked. — [Creator Docs (es)](https://create.roblox.com/docs/es-es/production/monetization/paid-random-items)
- [OFFICIAL] Analytics guidance: improve ARPDAU through conversion and ARPPU against similar-game benchmarks. — [Creator Docs: Monetization analytics](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/monetization)
- [COMMUNITY] Practitioner advice:
  - Avoid pay-to-win, which drives negative reviews and churn.
  - Launch with a best-guess price, watch sales for a few weeks, then adjust.
  - Layer at least three price points with an anchor.
  - Sources: [ugccraft](https://ugccraft.com/blog/roblox-game-passes-pricing-guide/); [creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-price-game-passes-roblox)

### Inferences
- Several platform moves look aligned with regulatory pressure: age checks feeding the 18+ DevEx rate, ending cross-game pass sales, the Transfers API, ID verification for 2D clothing sellers, and trading eligibility checks for paid random items. Roblox is also shifting monetization incentives toward verified adults.
- For a kid-heavy audience, the safest monetization mix leans on transparent passes, cosmetics, subscriptions and private servers. Randomized mechanics or luck boosts sold for Robux carry policy obligations and the most reputational risk.
